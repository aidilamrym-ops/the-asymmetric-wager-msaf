# Section 1: The Paradox of Actual Infinity and the Scale Solution of the Universe

## 1. The Pure Continuous Interval Paradox ($0 \to 0.5 \to 1$)
In classical mathematics, the interval between $0$ to $0.5$ and $0.5$ to $1$ is considered to have linear proximity with a constant interval:
$$\Delta = 0.5 - 0 = 0.5$$
$$1 - 0.5 = 0.5$$

However, if this domain adheres to the concept of **Actual Infinity** (*Actual Infinity / Absolute Continuum*), between the coordinates $0$ and $0.5$ there is an unbounded density of points ($X_0, X_1, \dots \to \infty$). Based on **Zeno's Paradox**, the process of computation or information transfer from $0$ to $0.5$ requires the completion of an unbounded number of stages.

**Epistemological Consequences:**
If the number of information stages is actually infinite, then the first step can never begin, and the exact values $0.5$ and $1$ logically **never exist** (cannot be achieved/confirmed within the reality of information).

## 2. The Scale Solution of the Observable Universe
To rescue the existence of the numbers $0.5$ and $1$, mathematics must be bound by the physical constants of reality that limit the upper and lower domains.

### A. Upper Bound (Macro Scale)
If the base interval $0$ to $1$ is mapped directly onto the diameter of the **Observable Universe**, its value is bounded finitely:
$$\text{Global Scale } [0, 1] \equiv D_{\text{obs}} \approx 8.8 \times 10^{26} \text{ meter}$$ <!-- [REF-PLANCK_2018_VI] [REF-PLANCK_2018_VI_ARXIV] -->

Then the critical coordinate value $0.5$ has a definite physical size:
$$\text{Coordinate } 0.5 = \frac{1}{2} D_{\text{obs}} \approx 4.4 \times 10^{26} \text{ meter}$$

### B. Lower Bound (Smallest Information Pixel)
The division of space or intervals cannot be performed indefinitely because it is bounded by the **Planck Scale** (the endpoint of the collapse of the laws of physics):
$$\ell_P = \sqrt{\frac{\hbar G}{c^3}} \approx 1.62 \times 10^{-35} \text{ meter}$$ <!-- [REF-NIST_CODATA_2022_TABLE] -->

### C. Conclusion of the Scale Formulation
By wedding the upper and lower bounds, the number of information steps ($N_{\text{steps}}$) from $0$ to $1$ is **Finite / Bounded**:
$$N_{\text{steps}} = \frac{D_{\text{obs}}}{\ell_P} \approx \frac{8.8 \times 10^{26}}{1.62 \times 10^{-35}} \approx 5.4 \times 10^{61} \text{ steps}$$ <!-- [REF-NIST_CODATA_2022_TABLE] [REF-PLANCK_2018_VI] [REF-PLANCK_2018_VI_ARXIV] -->

Because the number of steps is modular and finite ($5.4 \times 10^{61}$), the numbers $0.5$ and $1$ escape the trap of the infinity paradox, become real, have precise informational measures, and can be computed.
